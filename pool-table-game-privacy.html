Pool Table Game Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers the Pool Table Game app for Android and iOS, made by Thulo Technology ("we", "us"), published on Google Play as T Tech Game.

Information we collect

Pool Table Game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics in the game. The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or microphone.

Information kept on your device

The game saves a few things on your phone only, so it can remember them next time you play:

  • Your settings (sound, music, vibration, table cloth, shot clock, graphics quality)
  • Your points, level and number of frames played and won
  • Whether you have finished the first lesson

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for a short buzz when you hit the ball. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.

Third-party services

The game is built with the Unity engine and is distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. When you download or update the game, those stores handle the download under their own privacy policies.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not collect it from children.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, analytics or online play, we will update this page and show the new date before that version is released.
