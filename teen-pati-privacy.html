Teen Pati Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the Teen Pati card game app for Android, made by Thulo Technology ("we", "us"), published on Google Play as T Tech Game. It explains what the game does with information, in plain words.

In short

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • Your chips, record and settings stay on your own device.
  • Chips are play tokens with no cash value. There is no real-money gambling and nothing to buy.
  • The current version shows no ads and has no analytics.

Information we collect

Teen Pati does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The game does not ask for your email, contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Information kept on your device

The game saves a few things on your phone only, so it can remember them next time you play:

  • Your chip balance and the name you choose for friends tables
  • Your record: hands played, hands won, biggest win, trails and pure sequences
  • Your settings (sound, game speed) and your chosen game type, table and computer level

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes it.

Playing with friends

A friends table connects phones directly to each other over your own Wi-Fi or a phone's hotspot, using a table code. No internet server of ours is involved. The name you type and your game moves are sent only to the other phones at that table, while you play, and are not kept afterwards.

Permissions

The game uses network access only to connect phones at a friends table on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. It does not use any other permission to collect information.

Advertising

The current version shows no ads. The play screen has a space that shows the game's name, kept for a possible future ad. If a future version shows ads, they would come from Google AdMob, and before that version is released we will update this page to say exactly what is shared. In general, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your internet address, and basic information about how ads are shown, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the game would ask for your consent first, and you could reset or turn off ad personalisation at any time in your phone's settings. Google's practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Third-party services

The game is built with the Unity engine and is distributed through Google Play. When you download or update the game, Google Play handles the download under its own privacy policy. If the game crashes, Google Play may send an anonymous crash report to us under its own policy; it does not identify you.

Security

Because the game keeps your information only on your device and does not send it to us, there is nothing of yours on our servers to lose or leak.

Age

Teen Pati is a card game with simulated betting using play chips, meant for players aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You can see, change or delete everything the game saves by using its Settings, clearing its data, or uninstalling it. If you have any question about your information, write to us and we will answer.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, analytics or online play over the internet, we will update this page and show the new date before that version is released.
