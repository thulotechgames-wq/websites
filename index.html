About us

“Thulo” means big.
So do our dreams.

Thulo Technology is an independent game studio from Nepal. We are a small, focused team of developers and designers who believe great games don't need giant budgets — just a sharp idea, tight gameplay and a lot of play-testing.

We build fast, test with real players and polish what people love. Every game we ship is designed to be light, fair and fun on any phone.

  • Light games that run smoothly on every device
  • Fair, player-friendly monetisation
  • Regular updates driven by player feedback
