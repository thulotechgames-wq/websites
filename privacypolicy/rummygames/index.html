Rummy Card Game Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Thulo Technology

This policy explains what data the Rummy Card Game app for Android and iOS (com.thulotechnology.rummycard) handles, why, and the choices you have. In this policy, "we" means Thulo Technology, the developer of the app.

The short version: the game has no account and no real-money play. Your progress stays on your device. When you are online, the game sends usage data and session recordings to Google Firebase and Microsoft Clarity so we can improve it.

Data that stays on your device

The following is saved only on your device, and we do not receive it:

  • the name you enter for yourself and the names entered for Pass and Play
  • your coins, level, progress, items and game history
  • your settings, such as sound, haptics, turn timer and daily reminder

Coins are for fun only and have no cash value. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes this information.

Data sent to service providers

When your device is online, the app sends the following data to two service providers. Most of it does not identify you by name, but some of it, such as device identifiers and IP addresses, can count as personal data.

ProviderDataWhy we use it
Google Firebase Analytics Screens viewed and game events (such as rounds started and finished, level ups and tutorial completion), an app instance identifier, the advertising ID on Android where your device makes it available, device and app information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. To understand how the game is played and where it can improve.
Microsoft Clarity Session recordings of what happens on screen in the app, including taps and screen names, along with game events, device and app information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. To see where players get stuck and to find problems in the game.

We mask player names in Clarity session recordings, and we do not send player names to either provider as analytics data.

These providers process data under their own terms and may process it in countries other than yours. You can read the Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information and Microsoft Privacy Statement. How long these providers keep the data depends on their retention settings and terms.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not ask for your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not take payments in the app.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules it on your device and asks for notification permission. No server is involved. You can turn it off in the game's settings or in your device settings.

Feedback and ratings

If you choose to send feedback, the game opens your email app with a draft to info@thulotechnology.com. We receive what you send, including your email address, and use it only to read and reply to your feedback. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies.

Your choices

  • Play offline to stop data being sent during that time.
  • Uninstall the app to stop all further collection and remove the data saved on your device.
  • Email us at info@thulotechnology.com to ask a privacy question or to request access to, or deletion of, your data. Because the game has no account, we may need details from you, such as your feedback email address, to find any data linked to you, and we may not be able to match analytics data to you.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We rely on the safeguards of your device and our service providers to protect data. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
