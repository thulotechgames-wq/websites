Tic Tac Toe: Fade Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the Tic Tac Toe: Fade Quest app for Android and iOS ("the app"), made by Thulo Technology ("we", "us"), published on Google Play as T Tech Game. It explains what the game does with information, in plain words.

In short

  • No ads, no account, and we never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • We collect anonymous game statistics and crash reports to fix bugs and balance the bots.
  • If you send feedback, we receive what you write.

What we collect

  • Game events, such as levels started and finished, stars, mode, theme, and answers to the rating and difficulty prompts. We use them to see which levels are too hard or too easy and improve the game.
  • Crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, device model and operating system version. We use them to find and fix crashes.
  • App installation identifiers created by Firebase (not your name, phone number or advertising ID). We use them to count installs and crashes without knowing who you are.
  • Approximate location (country or city), worked out from your IP address by Google Analytics. The IP address itself is not stored. We use it to understand where players are.
  • Feedback you choose to send: a mood rating, tags, your message, the app version, your platform and basic game stats (stars, games played, wins, streak, current level, theme). We use it to read and act on your feedback.

Your progress, settings, stars and streak are stored only on your device.

Playing on the same Wi-Fi

When you host or join a game on the same Wi-Fi, the app uses your local network to find the other phone. The nickname you type and your moves go straight to that phone. They do not go to us or to any server, and we cannot see them. On iPhone you will be asked once for Local Network permission. Without it, this mode does not work.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules them on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Who processes the data

We use Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config and Cloud Firestore) as our service provider. Firebase processes the data above on our behalf. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or use it to track you across other apps.

Security and retention

Data sent to Firebase travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Firebase project (14 months at most), and is then deleted automatically. Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Feedback messages are kept until they are no longer needed to improve the app.

Your choices

  • Feedback is always optional.
  • Reminders are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time.
  • Settings → Reset progress clears the stars, levels and stats stored on your device.
  • To ask us to delete feedback you sent, or for any other privacy request, email info@thulotechnology.com.

Children

The app is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a feedback message, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
